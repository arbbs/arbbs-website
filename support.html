Account Information

ARBB Engineers is for authorised AR Broadband Service field engineers. Users cannot create an account inside the app. Engineer accounts are provisioned and managed by AR Broadband Service.

If you no longer require access, or need your engineer account reviewed, disabled, or removed, contact ARBBS Services using the support details on this page.

Privacy Policy General Contact

Before contacting support

  • Confirm you are using the email address issued or approved for your engineer account.
  • Use the Forgot / Reset Password option on the sign-in screen if you cannot remember your password.
  • If a job or visit is missing, contact the office so the assignment can be checked.
  • If photo upload or messaging fails, include the error message and your device type when contacting support.
